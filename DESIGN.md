# Sanborn visual system

The landing remains the incumbent visual authority: paper-green surfaces,
fern accents, Manrope headings, DM Sans text, and the original friendly
low-poly frog with large ivory eyes, highlights, toes and a curved smile.
Both pages use `src/frog.js` so mascot proportions remain identical.

The developer field manual extends this identity rather than rebranding it.
Its forest-dark specimen opening gives way to paper chapters with persistent
wayfinding. The system map uses real buttons, a text inspector and explicit
route states; the full Archify proposal remains separately inspectable.

Body text stays readable without animation. Motion is confined to the frog,
pauses outside the viewport, and respects reduced-motion preferences.
Mobile layouts stack the reading flow and present map components in a
two-column list backed by a complete text reference. Proposed capabilities,
unverified integrations and absent benchmark evidence must stay explicit.
